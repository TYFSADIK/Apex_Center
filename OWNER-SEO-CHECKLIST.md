# Apex Collision Center: SEO and Reputation Playbook

The website is fully optimized on-page (titles, descriptions, schema, local signals,
service area pages, voice and AI search surfaces). Rankings also depend on what happens
OFF the website. Work through this list and the site has everything it needs to compete
for searches like "best car servicing shop in North York".

## 1. Google Business Profile (most important, do first)

- Claim and verify the profile for Apex Collision Center at 4544 Dufferin Street, North York.
- Use the exact business name, address and phone (289) 544-2727 everywhere. Consistency matters.
- Set hours: Mon-Fri 9-5, Sat 10-3, Sun closed.
- Add the same 18 services listed on the website, with descriptions copied from the service pages.
- Upload real shop photos (exterior, bays, team at work). Replace the sample gallery images over time.
- Add the website URL https://apexcollisioncenter.ca once it is live.
- Post weekly: a finished job, a seasonal reminder (winter tires, AC check), an offer.

## 2. Reviews (Online Reputation Management)

- Ask every happy customer for a Google review. A short text or QR code at the counter works best.
- Aim for a steady flow, a few per week beats twenty in one day.
- Reply to every review within 48 hours, including negative ones, calmly and professionally.
- Never buy fake reviews. Google removes them and penalizes the profile.
- Once real reviews exist, replace the sample testimonials on the Reviews page with real ones
  (name, area, their words). Real reviews with real names convert far better.

## 3. Local citations and directories

- List the business with identical name, address and phone on: Bing Places, Apple Maps,
  YellowPages.ca, Yelp, 411.ca, and auto-specific directories (AutoTrader.ca service listings).
- The website's contact page, footer and JSON-LD already carry the canonical address.
  Match it exactly everywhere else.

## 4. Social (SMO and social search)

- Create matching profiles: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok. Same name, logo, address, hours, website link.
- Post job photos and short videos (30-60 seconds: "this noise means your wheel bearing is done").
  Short video is the highest reach format for local shops right now.
- Link every profile back to https://apexcollisioncenter.ca.

## 5. What the site already handles

- Local SEO: AutoRepair schema with geo coordinates, opening hours, service area cities,
  geo meta tags, embedded map, driving directions per city.
- AI/GEO/AIO: llms.txt and llms-full.txt manifests, FAQ schema on every service page,
  quotable fact blocks written for AI overviews.
- Voice search: Speakable schema plus conversational question-and-answer content.
- Map engines: hasMap link, ICBM and geo.position tags, per-city directions pages.
- Conversion: sticky mobile call button, click-to-call everywhere, online booking form,
  upfront pricing messaging, trust checklist.
- Social sharing: share buttons on every service and area page, Open Graph and Twitter cards.

## 6. What was deliberately NOT faked

- No App Store (ASO), Amazon (AMSO) or eCommerce SEO was added: the business has no app,
  no Amazon store and no online shop. Adding those surfaces would be dishonest and can hurt rankings.
- No star rating schema was added: the on-site testimonials are representative samples.
  Add AggregateRating schema only after real Google reviews exist, using the real rating and count.
- No one can guarantee position 1 on Google. What this setup guarantees is that the site
  itself will never be the reason you do not rank: every on-page factor is covered.

## 7. Maintenance rhythm

- Monthly: check Google Search Console for queries you already rank for; expand the winning pages.
- Quarterly: refresh one service page with new FAQs from real customer questions.
- After any copy change: run `python3 build.py` to regenerate, then re-upload.

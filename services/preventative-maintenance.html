Preventative Maintenance

Small services now prevent big repairs later.

Service detail

Small services now prevent big repairs later.

Preventative maintenance is simply doing the right service at the right mileage. Fluids, filters, belts and wear parts all have a service life, and replacing them on schedule costs a fraction of repairing the damage when they fail.

We follow your manufacturer maintenance schedule, not an upsell script. Every visit includes a written inspection report with photos of anything we find, prioritized by safety first, so you can plan instead of panic.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • You are near a 30, 60, 90 or 120k service milestone
  • It has been over a year since a full inspection
  • You bought a used car and want a baseline health check
  • A long road trip is coming up
  • Fluids look dark or levels keep dropping
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Schedule review

We check your manufacturer schedule against your mileage and service history to build the right service list.

Full inspection

A bumper to bumper inspection covers brakes, suspension, steering, belts, hoses, fluids, lights, tires and battery.

Scheduled services

We perform the due items: fluids, filters, spark plugs, coolant or transmission service as the schedule calls for.

Written report

You get a clear report with priorities: what is safety critical now, what to plan for soon, and what is fine.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Factory scheduled service items for your mileage milestone
  • Engine oil, filter and all fluid level checks and top ups
  • Brake pad and rotor measurement with the numbers in writing
  • Tire tread depth, pressure check and rotation where applicable
  • Belt, hose, battery and light inspection
  • Written report prioritized by safety so you can plan ahead

How long it takes

Most milestone services take 1 to 2 hours. Larger intervals with spark plugs or fluids can take half a day.

How pricing works

Priced from your manufacturer schedule, not a generic package. You see every line item and approve the list before we begin, and we never add services you did not ask for.

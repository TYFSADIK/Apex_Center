Electrical Diagnostic

From dead batteries to mystery drains, we trace it.

Service detail

From dead batteries to mystery drains, we trace it.

Electrical faults are the hardest problems to guess at. A light that flickers, a window that works sometimes, a battery that dies overnight, these are circuit problems that need systematic testing with meters and wiring diagrams, not parts swapping.

We test voltage drops, check grounds, load test circuits and hunt parasitic drains with the car asleep. Because we diagnose down to the wire, you replace only what has actually failed.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Battery dies overnight or needs frequent boosts
  • Lights dim, flicker, or some stop working
  • Power windows, locks or seats work intermittently
  • Blown fuses that keep blowing again
  • Burning plastic smell or warm wiring
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Symptom mapping

We reproduce the fault and map which circuits are involved using wiring diagrams for your exact vehicle.

Meter testing

Voltage, resistance and voltage drop tests narrow the fault to a wire, connector, ground or component.

Drain testing

For dead battery complaints we measure parasitic draw with the vehicle asleep and pull fuses to isolate the circuit.

Repair and verify

We repair the wiring or replace the failed component, then retest the full circuit under load to prove the fix.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Wiring diagram research for your exact year, make and model
  • Voltage, resistance and voltage drop testing on the suspect circuits
  • Ground and connector inspection, cleaning and repair
  • Parasitic drain testing with the vehicle fully asleep
  • Repair of the failed wire, terminal or component, not just parts swapping
  • Full circuit retest under load to prove the fix

How long it takes

Simple faults take 1 to 2 hours. Intermittent electrical issues and parasitic drains can take a half day of methodical testing.

How pricing works

Quoted by diagnostic time plus the actual repair. Many electrical faults are fixed with a proper wire or terminal repair, which costs far less than replacing a module.

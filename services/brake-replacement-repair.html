Brake Replacement and Repair

Stop shorter, drive safer, with complete brake service.

Service detail

Stop shorter, drive safer, with complete brake service.

Brakes are your car most important safety system, and they wear a little every time you drive. Pads thin out, rotors develop grooves and rust, calipers can stick, and brake fluid absorbs moisture over time which lowers its boiling point.

We service the complete system: pads, rotors, calipers, hoses, hardware and fluid. Every brake job is measured, machined or replaced to spec, then road tested and bedded so the pedal feels firm and stops are quiet from day one.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Squealing, squeaking or grinding when you brake
  • Brake pedal feels soft, spongy or sinks low
  • Car pulls to one side while braking
  • Vibration or pulsing through the pedal or steering wheel
  • Brake warning light is on, or stopping distances feel longer
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Measure everything

Pad thickness, rotor thickness and runout, caliper slide movement and fluid condition are all measured and recorded.

Replace or machine

Pads are replaced, rotors are machined or replaced to stay above minimum thickness, and sticking calipers are serviced or replaced.

Hardware and fluid

New hardware, shims and lubricant where required, plus a brake fluid flush if the fluid is old or contaminated.

Bed and road test

Pads are bedded to the rotors and the car is road tested to confirm a firm pedal, straight stops and no noise.

The fine print, up front

How long it takes

A standard pad and rotor service takes 2 to 3 hours. Caliper or brake line work can extend the visit.

How pricing works

Straight answers

Brake Replacement and Repair questions

If the rotor is thick enough and not cracked or severely rusted, machining restores a true surface for less money. We measure first and show you the numbers, then recommend whichever is right.

Light surface rust forms overnight, especially in humid weather, and wipes off after a few stops. Constant squealing while driving usually means the wear indicators are touching and the pads are due.

Typically 40,000 to 70,000 km depending on driving style, traffic and pad material. We measure pad thickness at every visit so you get a real answer for your car.

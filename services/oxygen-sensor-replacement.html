Oxygen Sensor Replacement

Fix the check engine light and get your mileage back.

Service detail

Fix the check engine light and get your mileage back.

Oxygen sensors tell your engine computer how to mix fuel and air. When a sensor gets slow or fails, the computer guesses, which means worse fuel economy, rough running, failed emissions tests and a check engine light that will not stay off.

We verify the sensor is truly the fault with live data, because wiring and exhaust leaks can set the same codes. The new sensor is installed with anti seize, torqued correctly, and the system is confirmed in closed loop on a road test.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Check engine light with an O2 sensor code
  • Fuel economy drops noticeably
  • Rough idle or hesitation
  • Rotten egg smell from the exhaust
  • Failed Drive Clean or emissions test
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Confirm with live data

We watch sensor waveforms and fuel trims to prove the sensor is slow or dead, not a wiring or exhaust leak issue.

Replace the sensor

The failed sensor is removed with the proper socket and a new quality sensor is installed to the correct torque.

Check the circuit

Heater circuit, wiring and connectors are tested so the new sensor is not condemned by a bad wire.

Verify operation

Codes are cleared and a road test confirms fuel trims return to normal and the light stays off.

Wheel Bearings Replacement

Silence the roar and keep your wheels rolling true.

Service detail

Silence the roar and keep your wheels rolling true.

Wheel bearings let your wheels spin freely while carrying the full weight of the car. When they wear, they roar, and the noise usually changes when you steer left or right. A badly worn bearing develops play that affects braking and handling.

We confirm the noisy bearing on the lift and with a road test, replace the hub assembly with a quality part, torque the axle nut to exact spec, and verify the noise is gone on a final drive.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Humming or roaring that gets louder with speed
  • Noise changes pitch when you steer left or right
  • Grinding noise from one wheel
  • ABS or traction light on with a wheel speed code
  • Steering wheel vibration at highway speed
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Isolate the bearing

Road test plus on lift stethoscope testing pinpoints which bearing is noisy, so we never replace the wrong side.

Replace the hub

The hub assembly is replaced with a quality unit. Sealed modern bearings are not serviceable, replacement is the correct fix.

Torque to spec

The axle nut is torqued to the exact manufacturer specification, which is critical for bearing life.

Verify quiet

A final road test confirms the roar is gone and ABS sensors read correctly.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Road test and stethoscope diagnosis to confirm which bearing is noisy
  • Quality hub assembly or bearing, pressed in with proper tools
  • ABS sensor and tone ring inspection where integrated
  • Torque specs followed on the axle nut and hub bolts
  • Brake components rechecked during reassembly
  • Road test to confirm the humming is gone

How long it takes

Usually 1.5 to 2.5 hours per wheel, depending on whether it is a bolt on hub or a pressed bearing.

How pricing works

Quoted per wheel. We diagnose the exact noisy corner first, so you only replace the bearing that has actually failed.

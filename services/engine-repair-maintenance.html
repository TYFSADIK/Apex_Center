Engine Repair and Maintenance

From tune ups to major engine work, done properly.

Service detail

From tune ups to major engine work, done properly.

The engine is a system of systems: fuel, air, spark, cooling and lubrication all have to work together. When one falls behind you get warning lights, oil leaks, overheating, noise or lost power, and small faults become expensive fast if ignored.

We handle the full range, from tune ups and gasket replacements to intake, cooling and internal mechanical repair. Every job starts with diagnosis, uses quality gaskets and parts, and ends with a road test and leak check.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Oil or coolant spots where you park
  • Engine overheats or temperature gauge climbs
  • Ticking, knocking or tapping noises from the engine
  • Rough idle, misfires or loss of power
  • Excessive exhaust smoke or strong fuel smell
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Diagnose first

Compression, leak down, smoke testing and computer diagnostics pinpoint whether the fault is fuel, spark, air, cooling or mechanical.

Upfront quote

You approve a clear written quote with parts and labour broken out before we disassemble anything.

Quality parts

We use quality gaskets, seals and components torqued to manufacturer spec, with new fluids where the job requires.

Test and verify

After assembly we pressure test, check for leaks, clear codes and road test to confirm the repair under real driving.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Full diagnosis with scan data, compression or leak down testing where needed
  • Gaskets, seals, belts and tune up components as diagnosed
  • Quality parts that meet or exceed manufacturer specifications
  • Torque specs followed on every fastener
  • Fluids topped up and leaks rechecked after repair
  • Road test and recheck before the car leaves

How long it takes

Tune ups take a few hours. Gasket and seal work ranges from half a day to multiple days depending on the engine. We give you a realistic timeline with the quote.

How pricing works

Every engine job starts with diagnosis and a written quote. Complex work is quoted with parts and labour separate, and we call before doing anything beyond the approved scope.

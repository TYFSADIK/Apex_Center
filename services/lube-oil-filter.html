Lube, Oil and Filter

Clean oil on schedule keeps your engine alive.

Service detail

Clean oil on schedule keeps your engine alive.

Engine oil is the lifeblood of your car. It lubricates moving parts, carries heat away from hot spots, and traps dirt before it can cause wear. Over time oil breaks down and the filter fills up, which is why regular changes are the single cheapest way to protect your engine.

At Apex Collision Center every oil service includes fresh quality oil matched to your manufacturer spec, a new oil filter, and a free multi point check of fluids, belts, lights and tire condition. You drive out knowing exactly where your car stands.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Oil change sticker mileage has passed, or it has been more than 6 months
  • Dashboard oil light or maintenance reminder is on
  • Oil on the dipstick looks dark, gritty or low
  • Engine sounds louder or rougher than usual
  • You notice a burning oil smell after driving
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Drain and inspect

We drain the old oil completely and check it for metal particles or coolant traces that can warn of bigger problems.

New filter, fresh oil

A new quality filter goes on, then fresh oil that meets your vehicle maker specification, filled to the exact level.

Lube and check

We lubricate chassis points where applicable and top up washer fluid, coolant and power steering fluid.

Courtesy inspection

Belts, hoses, lights, wipers, tire tread and brake pad thickness get a quick visual check, free with every oil change.

Reset and remind

We reset your maintenance light and place a reminder sticker so you always know when the next service is due.

Straight answers

Lube, Oil and Filter questions

Most modern cars need an oil change every 8,000 to 12,000 km or every 6 to 12 months, depending on the oil type and how you drive. Your owner manual has the exact interval. We set a reminder sticker so you never have to guess.

We follow your manufacturer specification. Most newer vehicles call for synthetic or synthetic blend oil because it resists breakdown far longer. We will explain the options and the price difference before we start.

A standard oil change takes about 30 to 45 minutes, including the free multi point inspection. You can wait in our lounge or drop the car and come back.

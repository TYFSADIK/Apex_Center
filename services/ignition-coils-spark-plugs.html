Ignition Coils and Spark Plugs Replacement

Smooth idle, strong acceleration, better mileage.

Service detail

Smooth idle, strong acceleration, better mileage.

Spark plugs fire thousands of times per minute to ignite the fuel mixture, and ignition coils supply the high voltage they need. Worn plugs and weak coils cause misfires, which feel like stumbling, shake the engine, waste fuel and can damage the catalytic converter.

We read misfire data to find the guilty cylinders, replace plugs with the correct type for your engine, and replace only the coils that test bad. The result is a smooth idle and the power your car was built with.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Engine misfires, stumbles or shakes at idle
  • Flashing check engine light
  • Hesitation or lack of power on acceleration
  • Hard starting
  • Fuel economy gets worse for no clear reason
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Find the misfire

Misfire counters and live data identify exactly which cylinders are affected and whether it is spark, fuel or compression.

Replace plugs

Plugs are replaced with the exact type and gap your manufacturer specifies, platinum or iridium where required.

Test coils

Each coil is tested. Only failed coils are replaced, so you never pay for parts that still work.

Verify smooth

Codes are cleared and a road test confirms no misfires, smooth idle and full power.

Straight answers

Ignition Coils and Spark Plugs Replacement questions

A flashing light means an active misfire that can overheat and destroy the catalytic converter within minutes. Drive gently to the nearest shop or call us right away.

We replace failed coils and test the rest. If the car has high mileage and multiple coils are weak, we will show you the data and let you decide.

Iridium and platinum plugs often last 100,000 to 160,000 km. Copper plugs much less. Your owner manual lists the interval for your engine.

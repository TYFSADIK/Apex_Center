Battery, Alternator and Starter Check

Start every morning with confidence.

Service detail

Start every morning with confidence.

Starting and charging problems all feel the same: click, slow crank, or nothing. But the fix could be a battery, an alternator, a starter, a corroded cable or a parasitic drain, and each costs very differently.

We test the whole system as a system. Battery load test, alternator output under load, starter draw, cable voltage drops and parasitic drain are all measured, so you replace exactly the failed part and nothing else.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Slow cranking, especially on cold mornings
  • Clicking sound when you turn the key
  • Battery warning light on the dash
  • Lights dim at idle but brighten when you rev
  • Needing a boost more than once
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Battery load test

The battery is tested under real load, not just voltage, to see if it can actually deliver cranking amps.

Charging test

Alternator output is measured at idle and under full electrical load to confirm it charges in all conditions.

Starter draw test

Starter current draw is measured to catch a failing starter before it strands you.

Cables and drains

Cable connections are cleaned and tested for voltage drop, and parasitic drain is checked if the battery keeps dying.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Battery load test and state of health report
  • Alternator output test under electrical load
  • Starter draw test and cranking voltage check
  • Cable, terminal and ground inspection and cleaning
  • Parasitic drain test if the battery keeps dying
  • Written summary of which component failed and why

How long it takes

A full starting and charging system test takes about 45 minutes to 1 hour. Drain testing can add time.

How pricing works

Testing is quoted upfront and you only replace the component that failed the test. Terminal cleaning and cable service are often all a car needs.

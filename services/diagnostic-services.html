Diagnostic Services

We find the real problem before we touch a wrench.

Service detail

We find the real problem before we touch a wrench.

Modern cars have dozens of computers watching every system. When the check engine light comes on, the car stores a code, but the code is only a starting point. A proper diagnosis connects the code to live sensor data and physical testing to find the actual failed part.

Our technicians use professional scan tools plus old fashioned inspection. We road test, read live data, and verify the fault before quoting any repair. You pay for answers, not guesses, and we explain the findings in plain language.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Check engine, ABS, airbag or traction control light stays on
  • Car hesitates, stalls, idles rough or lacks power
  • Strange noises, smells or vibrations you cannot explain
  • Fuel economy suddenly gets worse
  • A warning message appears on the dash
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Scan and record

We pull all stored and pending codes from every module, not just the engine computer, and record freeze frame data.

Live data and testing

We watch sensors in real time and run targeted tests on the suspect circuits and components.

Physical inspection

Wiring, connectors, vacuum lines and mechanical parts get a hands on check to confirm what the data suggests.

Clear report and quote

You get a written explanation of the fault, the repair options, and an upfront price before any work begins.

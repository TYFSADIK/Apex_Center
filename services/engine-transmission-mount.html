Engine and Transmission Mount Replacement

Stop the clunk and feel the smoothness again.

Service detail

Stop the clunk and feel the smoothness again.

Engine and transmission mounts hold your powertrain in place while absorbing its vibration. They are rubber and hydraulic components, and they crack, collapse and leak with age. Failed mounts let the engine rock, which you feel as clunks, vibration at idle and harsh shifts.

We inspect all mounts, replace the failed ones with quality parts, and support the powertrain properly during the job. Afterwards the car idles smooth and shifts without the thump.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Clunk when shifting into drive or reverse
  • Vibration at idle that smooths out when driving
  • Engine visibly rocks when revved
  • Thump on acceleration or deceleration
  • Cracked, collapsed or leaking mounts found on inspection
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Inspect all mounts

Every mount is checked for cracks, collapse, fluid leaks and excess movement with the engine loaded.

Support and replace

The powertrain is properly supported while failed mounts are replaced with quality parts.

Torque to spec

All fasteners are torqued to manufacturer specification with the drivetrain at normal ride height where required.

Verify smooth

We confirm no clunk on gear engagement, smooth idle and no new vibrations on the road test.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Diagnosis of the clunk with the car on the lift and under load
  • Inspection of all mounts, not just the obvious one
  • Quality replacement mounts matched to your vehicle
  • Proper torque specs and alignment of the powertrain
  • Related bushings and brackets checked while accessible
  • Road test through drive, reverse and idle to verify smoothness

How long it takes

Usually 2 to 4 hours depending on how many mounts are worn and how buried they are.

How pricing works

Quoted after we identify which mounts have failed. Replacing mounts as a matched set is quoted as an option when the rest are close behind.

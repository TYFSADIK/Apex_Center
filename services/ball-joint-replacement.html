Ball Joint Replacement

Tight steering and safe handling start here.

Service detail

Tight steering and safe handling start here.

Ball joints are the pivot points that connect your wheels to the suspension, letting the wheels steer and move up and down at the same time. They carry the weight of the car, so when they wear out you get looseness in the steering and, in extreme cases, a wheel that can separate.

We replace worn ball joints with quality parts, torque every fastener to manufacturer spec, and road test to confirm tight, quiet steering. If the control arm bushings are worn too, we will show you and let you decide.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Clunking or knocking over bumps, especially when turning
  • Steering feels loose or wanders on the highway
  • Uneven or feathered tire wear on the front tires
  • Vibration in the steering wheel
  • A technician notes play in the joint during inspection
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Lift and load test

With the car on the lift we check each joint for vertical and lateral play under load, the only reliable test.

Replace the joint

The worn joint is pressed or unbolted out and a new quality joint is installed, or the full control arm if that is the better repair.

Torque to spec

Every fastener is torqued to manufacturer specification with suspension loaded where required.

Road test

We road test over bumps and through turns to confirm the clunk is gone and steering is tight.

Straight answers

Ball Joint Replacement questions

A slightly worn joint should be replaced soon. A badly worn or separating joint is dangerous and can let the wheel fold under the car. If you hear loud clunking, get it checked right away.

CV Axle and Shaft Assembly Replacement

End the clicking and get power to the wheels.

Service detail

End the clicking and get power to the wheels.

CV axles deliver engine power to your front wheels while letting them steer and move with the suspension. Their joints are packed in grease under rubber boots. When a boot tears, grease flies out, dirt gets in, and the joint starts clicking on turns.

We replace the complete axle assembly with a quality unit, which is more reliable than rebooting a joint that has already run dry. New axle nuts are torqued to spec and the car is road tested through full lock turns.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Clicking or popping when turning sharply
  • Grease splattered on the inside of the wheel or under the car
  • Vibration under acceleration that smooths out when coasting
  • Clunk when shifting into drive or reverse
  • Torn or cracked CV boot spotted during inspection
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Confirm the axle

Full lock turns and on lift inspection confirm the clicking axle and check boot condition on both sides.

Replace the assembly

The complete axle is replaced with a quality unit, including new axle nut and fresh transmission seal where applicable.

Torque to spec

The axle nut is torqued to the exact manufacturer specification, critical for bearing and joint life.

Road test

Figure eights and highway runs confirm no clicking, no vibration and no leaks.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Road test and turn test to confirm which axle is clicking
  • Boot inspection for tears and grease loss
  • Quality complete CV axle assembly
  • Transmission seal inspection while the axle is out
  • Proper torque on the axle nut and suspension fasteners
  • Road test with sharp turns to verify the clicking is gone

How long it takes

Usually 1.5 to 2.5 hours per side.

How pricing works

Quoted per side with parts and labour separate. We replace the complete assembly rather than rebooting a worn joint, because it lasts longer and costs less in labour.

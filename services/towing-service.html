Stranded at 2 AM? We are already rolling.

Breakdowns do not keep office hours, so neither do we. Our towing service runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across North York, Toronto and the surrounding GTA. One call to (416) 661-6665 and a truck is dispatched.

We tow accident vehicles, breakdowns and non-runners straight to our shop at Unit 41 & 42, 4544 Dufferin Street, where our technicians can start diagnosing immediately, or to any address you choose.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Car broke down and will not restart
  • Accident damage that makes the car unsafe to drive
  • Flat tire with no usable spare
  • Overheating that forces you to stop
  • Any situation where driving further would cause more damage
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Call (416) 661-6665

Tell us where you are, what happened, and where you want the car to go.

Truck dispatched

The nearest available truck heads to you. We confirm the pickup location by phone.

Safe loading

Your car is loaded on a flatbed with proper tie-downs to prevent any transport damage.

Delivered

We bring it to our shop for immediate diagnosis or to the address of your choice.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • 24/7 dispatch, day, night, weekends and holidays
  • Flatbed and wheel lift trucks for cars, SUVs and light trucks
  • Careful loading with proper tie downs, no drag damage
  • Transport to our shop or the destination you choose
  • Accident scene response coordinated with you
  • Direct handoff to our service team if the car needs repair

How long it takes

We dispatch immediately when you call. Arrival time depends on your location and traffic, and we will give you an honest estimate on the phone.

How pricing works

Quoted by distance before the truck rolls, so there are no surprises. If we tow your car to our shop and you approve the repair, we will talk about the tow on the final bill.

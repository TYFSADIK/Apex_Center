Fuel Pump Replacement

Reliable starts and steady fuel pressure, restored.

Service detail

Reliable starts and steady fuel pressure, restored.

The fuel pump lives inside your gas tank and pushes fuel to the engine at high pressure. When it weakens, pressure drops and the engine starves, which shows up as long cranking, sputtering at speed, or a car that dies and will not restart until it cools down.

We confirm pump failure with fuel pressure and electrical testing before replacing anything, because the same symptoms can come from a relay, filter or wiring. The new pump module is tested under load before the car leaves.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Engine cranks a long time before starting
  • Car sputters or loses power at highway speed
  • Engine dies and restarts only after cooling down
  • Whining or humming from the rear fuel tank area
  • No start with fuel in the tank and a healthy battery
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Prove it is the pump

Fuel pressure is tested at the rail, and pump current and relay operation are checked electrically.

Safe replacement

The tank is accessed safely, the module assembly is replaced with new seals, and the tank and lines are inspected.

Filter and strainer

The pickup strainer is replaced and the system is checked for contaminated fuel that could kill a new pump.

Pressure verification

Final pressure and volume tests confirm the new pump delivers full spec under load.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Fuel pressure and volume testing before condemning the pump
  • Electrical testing of the pump circuit, relay and wiring
  • Quality replacement pump, with the tank strainer where applicable
  • Tank and lock ring inspection while open
  • Fuel pressure verification after installation
  • Road test including sustained highway speed

How long it takes

Typically 2 to 4 hours. Some vehicles need the tank dropped, others have an access panel that speeds the job.

How pricing works

We prove the pump is the problem with pressure testing first, so you never pay for a pump you did not need. The written quote covers the pump, related parts and labour.

Straight answers

Fuel Pump Replacement questions

Heat and low fuel level are the top killers. Fuel cools the pump, so habitually running near empty shortens its life. Contaminated fuel and old age are the other common causes.

We usually recommend the complete module assembly. It includes the sender, strainer and seals, and it is far more reliable than swapping only the pump motor.

Most fuel pump replacements take 2 to 4 hours depending on tank access. We will give you a firm time when you book.

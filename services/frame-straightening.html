Frame Straightening

Bent frame, measured and pulled back to factory spec.

Service detail

Bent frame, measured and pulled back to factory spec.

The frame is the skeleton your car is built on. Even a moderate impact can shift it by millimetres, and millimetres matter: a bent frame causes pulling, uneven tire wear, and doors that never quite close right again.

We anchor your car to a frame bench and use computerized measuring to compare every point against factory specifications, then hydraulic pulls bring it back. We re-measure after every pull and show you the before and after readings.

Warning signs

How to tell you need it

  • Car pulls to one side after an accident
  • Uneven or rapid tire wear following a collision
  • Doors, hood or trunk that do not align after impact
  • Visible bending or wrinkling in frame rails or aprons
  • Another shop told you the frame might be bent
Noticing any of these? Book an inspection and we will confirm what is going on before you spend a dollar on repairs.
Our process

How we do it

Computerized measurement

Electronic measuring compares your frame against factory specs and maps exactly where it moved.

Anchoring

The car is locked to the frame bench so pulls move only the damaged area.

Hydraulic straightening

Controlled pulls bring each point back to specification, re-measured after every pull.

Verification

Final measurements confirm the frame matches factory spec before body panels go back on.

The fine print, up front

What is included, how long it takes, how we price it

What is included

  • Computerized frame measurement against factory specifications
  • Written report showing every point that is out of spec
  • Hydraulic straightening on the frame bench
  • Re-measurement to confirm the frame is back within tolerance
  • Suspension mounting point verification
  • Alignment check recommended after straightening

How long it takes

Measurement and assessment take a few hours. Straightening time depends on the severity and is quoted after measurement.

How pricing works

Measurement comes first and is quoted separately, so you know exactly what is bent before committing to the straightening work.
